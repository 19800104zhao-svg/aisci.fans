## 1. Notable references

### Evidence

- FutureHouse launched a public platform of science-specific agents focused on full-text literature analysis and specialized chemistry workflows. It explicitly positions chained agents as a way to accelerate discovery rather than just summarize papers.
  Source: https://www.futurehouse.org/research-announcements/launching-futurehouse-platform-ai-agents
- Arc Institute announced the first 2026 AIxBio Fellows cohort and disclosed that it received 300+ applications. The selected teams are working on projects like context-aware genomics, reinforcement-learning drug combinations, and orchestration around Arc's STATE model.
  Source: https://arcinstitute.org/news/aixbio-fellows-cohort-announcement-2026
- OpenAlex is pushing funding metadata into the scholarly graph and turned awards into first-class graph objects in 2026. It also launched affiliation curation for member institutions.
  Sources: https://blog.openalex.org/ and https://api.openalex.org/awards
- Semantic Scholar continues to expose a developer-first academic graph with recommendations, datasets, and paper/author/citation entities that downstream products like Litmaps and Connected Papers build on.
  Source: https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api
- OpenProtein.AI was selected for DARPA's NODES program to model protein function through structural dynamics, not just static structure prediction.
  Source: https://www.openprotein.ai/publications/openprotein-ai-selected-for-darpa-program/
- Isomorphic Labs announced a $2.1B Series B on 2026-05-12 to scale its AI drug design engine and pipeline.
  Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/isomorphic-labs-secures-2-1-billion-funding-to-scale-its-ai-drug-design-engine-302769674.html

### Inference

- AISci should copy the "graph + agent + human review" pattern, not the "chatbot first" pattern.
- AISci should avoid pretending to be a full autonomous scientist today. The market is rewarding infrastructure, reviewability, and tight domain loops.
- AISci should reinterpret "young talent discovery" as an institution-grade signal layer: fellowships, proof-of-work, structured claims, and graph-native identity.

## 2. Frontier problems and bottlenecks worth adding

### Evidence

- AutoResearchBench shows that scientific literature discovery remains a hard benchmark for AI agents and is still far from solved in a reliable way.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25256
- MADE argues that materials-discovery evaluation is still too static and introduces closed-loop environments with constrained oracle budgets.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20996
- CellVoyager shows concrete traction for autonomous scRNA-seq analysis agents in a notebook environment, including expert-rated novel findings.
  Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6
- Arc's Stack and OpenProtein's NODES work both point to the same bottleneck: modeling dynamic biological context, not just static sequence or structure.
  Sources: https://arcinstitute.org/news/foundation-model-stack and https://www.openprotein.ai/publications/openprotein-ai-selected-for-darpa-program/

### Inference

- AISci should add at least four problem families to the public graph soon:
  1. Agentic literature discovery and verification
  2. Closed-loop materials discovery
  3. Virtual cell and perturbation reasoning
  4. Protein function from dynamics
